Check that a backup has completed before changing phones, and keep a copy of records you need. See the privacy policy and account deletion guidance.

You can report content and block members. Moderators may hold or remove content, review public headshots, and suspend access where rules are breached. Contact skythorndigital@gmail.com to raise a concern or ask for a moderation decision to be reviewed. Your private evidence portfolio is separate from your community profile and posts.
