Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 August 2026

PM Career Proof is designed to keep your career record private and under your control.

Career data

Your roles, targets, capability assessments, evidence, credentials, manager feedback and history are stored on your device. PM Career Proof does not require an account to use the core app.

Backups and sharing

If you create a backup, PDF or other export, you choose where that file goes. Once you share or save a file outside the app, the destination service you choose may process it under its own privacy terms.

Subscriptions

Subscription purchases are processed by Apple. PM Career Proof uses RevenueCat to determine whether Pro access is active. RevenueCat may process purchase information and app or device identifiers needed to provide subscription status. Payment card details are not collected by PM Career Proof.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, they are scheduled as local notifications on your device. You can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings.

Sensitive information

Do not record confidential employer, client, security-sensitive or commercially sensitive information unless you are authorised to do so. The app is intended for career evidence, not confidential project records.

Sale of data

Skythorn Digital Ltd does not sell your personal data.

Support

For privacy or support questions, use the PM Career Proof support page. Do not include confidential or sensitive project information in a public support request.
