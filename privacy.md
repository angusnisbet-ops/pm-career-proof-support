# PM Career Proof Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 21 August 2026**

PM Career Proof is designed to keep your career information private and under your control.

## Information you add to the app

Career goals, capability assessments, evidence, credentials, notes and other career information you enter into PM Career Proof are stored locally on your device. The app does not require an account and this information is not sent to Skythorn Digital Ltd or uploaded to a PM Career Proof server.

You should avoid entering confidential employer, client, project or commercially sensitive information unless you are permitted to keep it on your personal device.

## Subscriptions and purchases

PM Career Proof offers optional auto-renewable subscriptions through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes payments. PM Career Proof does not receive or store your payment card or bank details.

RevenueCat is used to manage subscription status and unlock Pro features. RevenueCat may process purchase and subscription history together with an anonymous app user identifier and related App Store transaction information. This is used to provide subscription functionality and understand subscription performance. PM Career Proof does not use this information for advertising or cross-app tracking.

## Analytics, advertising and tracking

PM Career Proof does not include third-party advertising and does not use advertising trackers. The app does not sell your personal data.

## Notifications

If you enable reminders, notification settings are handled on your device. You can change notification permissions in iOS Settings.

## Your choices and deleting data

You can remove information you have entered in the app using its delete/reset controls, or remove the app from your device. Subscription management and cancellation are handled through your Apple Account settings.

## Third-party services

Subscription purchases are subject to Apple’s privacy practices. Subscription management is provided using RevenueCat. Their respective privacy policies apply to information they process on their systems.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if PM Career Proof changes how it handles data. The latest version will be published on this page.

## Contact

For privacy questions or support, visit the [PM Career Proof support page](https://angusnisbet-ops.github.io/pm-career-proof-support/).
