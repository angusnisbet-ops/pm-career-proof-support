PM Career Proof

Support

PM Career Proof is a career progression app built specifically for project managers.

Need help?

If something is not working, or you have a question about the app, open a support request on GitHub.

Open a support request

Please do not include confidential employer, project, client or commercially sensitive information in a support request.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are purchased and managed through Apple. You can restore purchases from the Pro screen in the app and manage or cancel a subscription from your Apple ID subscription settings.

Your career record

Your career record is stored on your device. Use Backup and Restore in the app to create a private backup file before replacing or resetting your phone.
