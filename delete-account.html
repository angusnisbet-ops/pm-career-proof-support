Delete your PM Career Proof account

In the iPhone or Android app, open Profile, My account, then Delete account. Export anything you need first.

Request deletion without the app

Email skythorndigital@gmail.com from your account email address with the subject “PM Career Proof account deletion”. We will verify account ownership before processing the request. If you use Apple’s private email relay, tell us that you signed in with Apple. Do not include your password, sign-in codes or career evidence.

What is removed

Your account, cloud evidence portfolio, profile, headshot and authored community content are removed. Questions or replies may leave a “Deleted by author” placeholder so moderation records remain understandable. Support and financial records may be retained for service, accounting and legal obligations; account references are removed where possible.

Files you exported, device backups and records held on another phone are separate copies. You can erase the current phone’s portfolio in Privacy and data.

Your subscription

Account deletion does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel it in the store where you purchased it to stop future renewals.
